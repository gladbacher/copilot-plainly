Playbook

Plan a piece of work

Have Copilot interview you, then attack the plan it helps you write.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Let it ask the questionsStart with the prompt below. Answering five questions beats writing a long brief.
  2. Get the planAsk for steps, owners, risks and the first week.
  3. Attack itAsk for the three weakest assumptions.
  4. Keep it aliveChoose Edit in Pages under the plan so you and your colleagues can keep updating it (Microsoft, 2026ba).
Prompt · Chat
I need to plan [piece of work]. The deadline is [date] and the people involved are [who].
Ask me up to five questions, one at a time, to understand it.
Then write a plan: steps in order, who does each, the main risks, and what I should do in the first week.

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Ground it in what existsName the project. Copilot can read the emails, files and meetings about it (Microsoft, 2026a).
  2. Get the planAsk for steps, owners, risks and the first week.
  3. Attack itAsk for the three weakest assumptions.
Prompt · Premium
I need to plan [piece of work]. Use my emails, meetings and files about [project] for context.
Write a plan: steps in order, who does each, the main risks, and what I should do in the first week.
Say where you are assuming something the sources do not confirm.

Push it further

The first answer is a draft. Send one of these next:

Follow-up
Act as a sceptical colleague. What are the three weakest assumptions?
Follow-up
What could go wrong in the first month, and what would I see first?
Follow-up
Turn this into a one-page update for my manager.

Before you use it

  • Owners and dates are suggestions until the people agree.
  • Check assumptions against what you know that Copilot does not.
  • Keep the plan somewhere you will update it.
