Quickstart · 2 minutes

Start here

Three steps. You finish knowing what you have and with one prompt that gives you something useful.

The short version

  • Go to copilot.cloud.microsoft and sign in with your work account, not a personal one.
  • Read the label under your name. It tells you which track to follow: Chat or Premium.
  • Give Copilot a goal, who it is for, and the format you want. Then refine it.
  1. Sign in with your work account Open copilot.cloud.microsoft. Use the email address your employer gave you. In the work version you should see the word Work under your profile and a green data protection shield. Personal accounts look different and have separate chats, files and protections (Microsoft, 2026a).
  2. Read your label Under your name you will see one of three labels: Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Basic) or M365 Copilot (Premium) (Microsoft, 2026b). The first two are the Chat track on this site. The third is the Premium track. If you see no label, use Microsoft's licence details page or see Troubleshooting.
  3. Send one good prompt Pick the prompt for your track below, change the brackets, and send it. If the first answer is not right, say what to change. Refining is normal.

Your first prompt

Microsoft says a prompt has four parts: a goal (the only required one), context, a source and your expectations (Microsoft, 2026c). This one uses three of them and works on both tracks.

Prompt
I need to explain [topic or decision] to [who: my manager / a client / my team].
They know [what they already know] and care about [what they care about].
Write a first draft in under 150 words, in a friendly and direct tone.
Then list the three questions they are most likely to ask.

ChatNext, on the Chat track

Copilot does not go looking through your files for you. To use a document, upload it or select it with your prompt. See giving it your files.

Prompt · Chat track
Using only the document I have attached, summarise it in five bullet points for [who].
If something is not in the document, say so instead of guessing.

  • Copilot Chat
  • Prompt library for Chat

PremiumNext, on the Premium track

Copilot can search your emails, files and meetings for you, within what you already have permission to open. You can name a file instead of uploading it.

Prompt · Premium track
Look at my emails and meetings from the last week about [project].
Summarise what has been decided, what is still open, and anything waiting on me.
Name the source of each point.

  • Copilot Chat and work data
