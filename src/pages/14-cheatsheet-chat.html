Cheat sheet · prints on one A4 page

ChatCopilot Chat on one page

Print with Ctrl+P or Cmd+P. Menus and footer are left out. Premium user? Use the Premium sheet.

Where to open it

  • copilot.cloud.microsoft
  • The Copilot icon in Edge
  • Inside Outlook and Teams
  • Sign in with your work account. Look for Work and the green shield.

What it can see

  • The web
  • What you paste, upload or have open in Outlook, Teams or Edge
  • Your inbox and calendar, inside Outlook
  • Not your files, chats or meetings by itself

The four-part recipe

Goal (required) · Context (who it is for) · Source (paste or attach it) · Expectations (format, tone, length).

Five prompts to copy

  1. "Using only the text below, summarise it in five bullets for [who]. If something is not in the text, say so."
  2. "Draft a reply to the email below. I want to [agree / decline]. Under 100 words, one clear next step."
  3. "From these notes, list the actions as a table: action, owner, due date. Write 'not stated' if missing."
  4. "Summarise my unread emails into: needs a reply, for information, can wait." (in Outlook)
  5. "I want help with [task]. Ask me up to five questions, one at a time, then write the best prompt."

Follow-ups that fix most answers

  • "Shorter, less formal, and put the recommendation first."
  • "If it is not in the source, say 'not stated'. Do not guess."
  • "Act as a sceptical reviewer. List the three weakest points, then fix them."
  • "Show where each claim comes from."

Features worth finding

  • Edit in Pages: keep and share a good answer
  • Custom instructions: set your preferences once
  • Notebooks: up to 50 files for one piece of work
  • Edge: summarise the page you are on

Agents to try

  • Prompt Coach: sharpens your prompts
  • Writing Coach: tone, grammar, style
  • Visual Creator: descriptions into visuals

Which agents you see can vary. Details.

Before you use the answer

  • Check names, numbers, dates and quotes against the source.
  • Never paste passwords, keys or data your organisation restricts.
  • Your chats are stored and your employer can search them.
  • See something you shouldn't have access to? Don't use it. Report it.
