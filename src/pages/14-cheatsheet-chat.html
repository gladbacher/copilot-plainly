Cheat sheet · print me (one A4 page)

ChatCopilot Chat on one page

Print this page with Ctrl+P or Cmd+P. Menus and footer are hidden when you print. Premium user? Use the Premium sheet.

Where to open it

  • copilot.cloud.microsoft
  • The Copilot icon in the Edge sidebar
  • Inside Outlook and Teams
  • Sign in with your work account. Look for Work and the green shield.

What it can see

  • The web
  • What you paste, upload or open beside it in Outlook or Teams
  • Not your inbox, files or meetings by itself, except in Outlook

The four-part recipe

Goal (required) · Context (who it is for) · Source (paste or attach it) · Expectations (format, tone, length). Then refine: "shorter", "less formal".

Five prompts to copy

  1. "Using only the text below, summarise it in five bullets for [who]. If something is not in the text, say so."
  2. "Draft a reply to the email below. I want to [agree / decline]. Under 100 words, one clear next step."
  3. "From these notes, list the actions as a table: action, owner, due date. Write 'not stated' if missing."
  4. "Act as a sceptical colleague. Tell me the three weakest assumptions in my plan."
  5. "I want help with [task]. Ask me up to five questions, one at a time, then write the best prompt."

Agents to try

  • Prompt Coach: sharpens your prompts
  • Writing Coach: tone, grammar, style
  • Visual Creator: turns descriptions into visuals

Which agents you see can vary. Details.

Safety basics

  • Check names, numbers and quotes
  • Never paste passwords, keys or restricted data
  • Your chats are stored and your employer can search them
  • Don't use a file you shouldn't see; report it
