Cheat sheet · print me (one A4 page)

PremiumM365 Copilot on one page

Print this page with Ctrl+P or Cmd+P. Menus and footer are hidden when you print. Chat user? Use the Chat sheet.

Where to open it

  • copilot.cloud.microsoft
  • The Copilot icon in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams
  • Sign in with your work account. Look for Work and the green shield.

What it can see

  • The web, and your emails, files, meetings, chats and calendar
  • Only what you can already open
  • Name a file or person; no upload needed

The four-part recipe

Goal (required) · Context (who it is for) · Source (name a file, person or project) · Expectations (format, tone, length). Then refine: "shorter", "less formal".

Five prompts to copy

  1. "Catch me up on [project] from the last two weeks: decisions, blockers, what is waiting on me. Name the source of each point."
  2. "Using only /[file], summarise it for a manager who must decide [what]. Say what is missing."
  3. "Prepare me for my meeting with [person]. Use our recent emails and files. Agenda, three questions, two objections."
  4. "Summarise this meeting for someone who missed it: decisions, actions with owners, open questions."
  5. "Draft a reply to the open email. I want to [agree / decline]. Under 100 words, one clear next step."

Agents to try

  • Researcher: cited reports from web and work sources
  • Analyst: data to insights and charts
  • Prompt Coach: sharpens your prompts

Which agents you see can vary. Details.

Safety basics

  • Check names, numbers and quotes
  • Never paste passwords, keys or restricted data
  • Share files with specific people, not everyone
  • See something you shouldn't? Don't use it; report it
