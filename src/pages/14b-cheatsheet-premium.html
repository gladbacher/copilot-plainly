Cheat sheet · prints on one A4 page

PremiumM365 Copilot on one page

Print with Ctrl+P or Cmd+P. Menus and footer are left out. Chat user? Use the Chat sheet.

Where to open it

  • copilot.cloud.microsoft
  • The Copilot icon in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams
  • The Copilot icon in Edge
  • Sign in with your work account. Look for Work and the green shield.

What it can see

  • The web
  • Your emails, files, meetings, chats and calendar
  • Only what you can already open
  • Name a file or person. No upload needed.

The four-part recipe

Goal (required) · Context (who it is for) · Source (name a file, person or project) · Expectations (format, tone, length).

Five prompts to copy

  1. "Catch me up on [project] from the last two weeks: decisions, blockers, what is waiting on me. Name each source."
  2. "Using only /[file], summarise it for a manager who must decide [what]. Say what is missing."
  3. "Prepare me for my meeting with [person]. Use our emails and files. Agenda, three questions, two objections."
  4. "Summarise this meeting for someone who missed it: decisions, actions with owners, open questions."
  5. "Draft a reply to the open email. I want to [agree / decline]. Under 100 words, one clear next step."

Follow-ups that fix most answers

  • "Shorter, less formal, and put the recommendation first."
  • "If it is not in the source, say 'not stated'. Do not guess."
  • "Act as a sceptical reviewer. List the three weakest points, then fix them."
  • "Show where each claim comes from."

Features worth finding

  • Edit in Pages: keep and share a good answer
  • Custom instructions: set your preferences once
  • Notebooks: up to 300 sources, including meetings
  • Scheduled prompts: a Monday summary, automatically

Agents to try

  • Researcher: cited reports from web and work sources
  • Analyst: data to insights and charts
  • Prompt Coach: sharpens your prompts

Which agents you see can vary. Details.

Before you use the answer

  • Check names, numbers, dates and quotes against the source.
  • Never paste passwords, keys or data your organisation restricts.
  • Your chats are stored and your employer can search them.
  • See something you shouldn't have access to? Don't use it. Report it.
