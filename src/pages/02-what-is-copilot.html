Guide 1

What Copilot is, and what it costs

"Copilot" is the name on many different Microsoft products. This page sorts out the ones you meet at work.

The short version

  • There are four work experiences: a free chat, a basic in-app version, the full paid add-on, and extras billed by usage.
  • The main difference is whether Copilot can reach your work data on its own.
  • Microsoft recently renamed things. Old and new names are both in use.
  • Your employer buys and assigns the paid licence, not you.

1The names changed

Microsoft's own documentation now says that Microsoft 365 Copilot is called Microsoft Copilot, and Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is called Microsoft Copilot Chat. Pages and menus still use the old names during the transition (Microsoft, 2026a). The web address is now copilot.cloud.microsoft.

In practice: if one article says "Microsoft 365 Copilot" and another says "Microsoft Copilot" they are usually describing the same product. Do not mix it up with the free consumer Copilot or with GitHub Copilot, which is for programmers. Those are separate.

Documentation and reports disagree on one point

Microsoft's overview says Basic users have standard access to Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote. Several university IT help pages say that embedded Copilot stopped for people without a paid licence in April 2026. I could not settle this from Microsoft's pages. Trust what you see in your own apps, and ask IT if the Copilot button is missing. See Troubleshooting.

3What it can see

Copilot is a language model plus your work data plus the apps. How much of your work data it uses is the real difference between versions.

  • Basic versions cannot use Microsoft Graph, which is the layer that holds your emails, files, meetings and calendar. They can use your content only if you hand it over: paste it, upload a file, or open it next to Copilot in Outlook or Teams.
  • Premium pulls from Graph and Work IQ automatically. You can name a file to focus on, but you do not have to upload it. An administrator can switch Work IQ off, in which case answers are not enriched with your work context (Microsoft, 2026a).
  • Either way, Copilot only shows you data you already have at least view permission to open (Microsoft, 2026d). See Safety.

4The cost reality check

You probably do not pay for any of this directly.

Copilot Chat is included at no extra cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans when you sign in with a work or school account (Microsoft, 2026e). The paid licence is an add-on that your employer buys and an administrator assigns.

If you work for a small business, or you are the person deciding, these are Microsoft's listed prices for the Copilot Business add-on. They are US dollars per user per month, excluding tax, checked on 16 October 2026 (Microsoft, 2026f):

Prices for larger enterprise plans are on Microsoft's Copilot page. I have not listed them here because I could not confirm them on a Microsoft page.

  2. Is my licence assigned? (Microsoft publishes a licence checker at aka.ms/CopilotLicenseDetails.)
  3. Are web search and agents turned on for us?
  4. Do we have a company policy for what can be shared with Copilot?
