Playbook

Catch up after time away

An hour of scrolling, replaced by three prompts.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Start in OutlookCopilot there can summarise your inbox and check your calendar (Microsoft, 2026l).
  2. Then each busy Teams chatOpen the chat or channel, then ask Copilot Chat about what you have open (Microsoft, 2026a).
  3. Make one listPaste the summaries into Copilot Chat and ask for a single to-do list.
Prompt · Chat
I have been away since [date]. Summarise the emails I received since then.
Group them into: decisions made without me, things waiting on me, and things I can ignore.
Then list my meetings for the next [three] days and what I should read before each.

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Ask onceCopilot can read across your emails, meetings, chats and files (Microsoft, 2026a).
  2. Go project by projectFollow up with one prompt per project for detail.
  3. Ask what is waiting on youThen do those first.
Prompt · Premium
I have been away since [date]. Catch me up using my emails, meetings, Teams chats and files.
Give me: decisions made without me, things waiting on me, and what changed on [project].
Name the source of each point and put the most urgent first.

Push it further

The first answer is a draft. Send one of these next:

Follow-up
Draft a short "I am back" message to [person] that picks up the open points.
Follow-up
What did I miss in the [meeting name] meeting?
Follow-up
Which of these can I delegate?

Before you use it

  • Open the source for anything marked urgent.
  • It only sees what you can open. Ask a colleague what happened off the record.
  • Meeting answers need a transcript. See the limits.

Results vary by licence and by your files. If a prompt misses, reply to Copilot with what to change.
