The Copilot way

Friends and articles talk about what they do with other AI tools. Your workplace gives you Copilot. Here is the nearest route for each thing, on your licence.

The short version

  • Most everyday jobs have a Copilot route on both licences.
  • Chat users get there by handing Copilot the material. Premium users can let it find the material.
  • The less obvious ones are explained in Power features.

ChatOn Copilot Chat

You've heard of…The Copilot way
Chatting with an AI about anythingCopilot Chat at copilot.cloud.microsoft, signed in with your work account (Microsoft, 2026e).
Uploading a document and asking about itUpload or select the file with your prompt (Microsoft, 2026a). See Summarise a long document.
A slower "thinking" mode for hard questionsChoose Think deeper in the model selector (Microsoft, 2026a).
Web research with sourcesAsk for cited findings in ordinary chat. See Research a topic. The Researcher agent needs a Copilot licence (Microsoft, 2026ar).
A custom assistant set up for one jobAn agent. Look for New agent in the Copilot app. On Chat it can use instructions and websites (Microsoft, 2026ac). See Make your own agent.
Help analysing dataPaste a small table or upload the file and ask for trends and formulas. See Make sense of a spreadsheet.
Making imagesThe Visual Creator agent, where your organisation has it (Microsoft, 2026n).
An AI that reads your emailCopilot in Outlook works with your inbox and calendar on this licence (Microsoft, 2026l). See Clear my inbox.
A canvas or document that grows out of the chatCopilot Pages: Edit in Pages under any answer (Microsoft, 2026ba). See Power features.
A project space that holds your filesCopilot Notebooks, with up to 50 sources on this licence (Microsoft, 2026bb). See Power features.
An AI that remembers your preferencesMemory and custom instructions (Microsoft, 2026bh, 2026be). See Power features.
Summarising the web page you are onThe Copilot icon in Edge (Microsoft, 2026a).
A writing or prompt coachWriting Coach and Prompt Coach agents, via All agents (Microsoft, 2026n).
