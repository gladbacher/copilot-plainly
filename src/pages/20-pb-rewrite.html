Playbook

Shorten or sharpen

You have the words. Copilot is quick at cutting them down and changing the tone.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Paste the text into Copilot ChatAnything you are allowed to share. See what not to paste.
  2. Say who it is forAudience and length matter more than "make it better".
  3. Ask what it removedSo nothing important disappears quietly.
Prompt · Chat
Rewrite the text below for [audience] in about half the length.
Keep every number, date and name. Use plain words and short sentences.
After the rewrite, list anything you removed that they might still ask about.

Before you use it

  • Numbers and dates survived the cut.
  • The meaning has not shifted. Shorter text can sound more certain than you are.
  • Nothing confidential went into the wrong Copilot. Look for Work and the green shield.
