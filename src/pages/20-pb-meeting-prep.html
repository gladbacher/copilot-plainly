Prepare for a meeting

Walk in knowing your goal, your questions and what the other side will push back on.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Open Copilot in OutlookIt can answer questions about your calendar and meetings on the Chat licence (Microsoft, 2026l).
  2. Give it the backgroundPaste the invite text, the last email, or your own notes.
  3. Ask for a one-page briefUse the prompt below.
Prompt · Chat
I have a [30]-minute meeting with [who] about [topic]. My goal is [outcome].
Background:
[Paste any background here]
Give me a timed agenda, three questions I should ask, and two objections I should be ready for. Keep it to one page.

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Ask from anywhereCopilot Chat, Outlook or Teams. It can read the emails, chats and files behind the meeting (Microsoft, 2026a).
  2. Name the person or the meetingIt finds the history for you.
  3. Ask what you owe themThe most useful line in the prompt below.
Prompt · Premium
Prepare me for my meeting with [person] on [day]. Use our recent emails, chats and shared files.
Give me: a one-line goal, where we left things, three questions to ask, two objections to expect, and anything I promised and have not done.
Name the source of each point.

Push it further

The first answer is a draft. Send one of these next:

Follow-up
Draft a two-line message I can send beforehand to set the agenda.
Follow-up
Play the other side. What will they want from this meeting?
Follow-up
What decision should we leave the room with?

Before you use it

  • Check "where we left things" against your own memory.
  • Anything it says you promised: open the source.
  • The agenda is a draft. Cut it to fit the time.

Results vary by licence and by your files. If a prompt misses, reply to Copilot with what to change.
