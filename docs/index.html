Unofficial · for people who use Copilot at work

Get everything out of the Copilot you've got.

Copilot is the AI your workplace gives you. This is how to make it pull its weight: the best route for your licence, prompts you fill in and copy, and what to check before you trust the answer.

First: which Copilot do you have?

Go to copilot.cloud.microsoft, sign in with your work account and read the label under your name.

  • Copilot Chat (Basic) or M365 Copilot (Basic): follow the Chat sections on each page.
  • M365 Copilot (Premium): follow the Premium sections.

Start here walks you through it.

What do you want to get done?

  • Clear my inboxTriage unread mail, draft replies, flag what can wait.
  • Write a first draftFrom rough notes to a structured draft you can edit.
  • Shorten or sharpenMake existing text shorter, clearer or better pitched.
  • Summarise a long documentGet the point, the risks and the gaps from a long file.
  • Prepare for a meetingAgenda, questions and likely objections in five minutes.
  • Follow up a meetingDecisions, actions and owners, sent while it is fresh.
  • Make sense of a spreadsheetTrends, outliers and formulas without breaking the file.
  • Build a deckA structured first deck from a prompt or a document.
  • Research a topicA sourced answer you can check, not a confident guess.
  • Plan a piece of workSteps, risks and a first week, pressure-tested.
  • Learn something fastA personal tutor for a topic, a tool or a piece of jargon.
  • Catch up after time awayBack from leave: what happened, what is waiting on you.

How this site works

  • You are the pilot. Copilot drafts, searches and summarises. You decide, check and send.
  • Facts come from Microsoft's own pages. Each guide says when it was last checked, and says so where something could not be confirmed.
  • Nothing here needs admin rights. Where only an administrator can change something, the guide says so and shows how to check your own situation.
  • Read Safety and privacy before you paste anything sensitive.
