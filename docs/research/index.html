Playbook

Research a topic

Make Copilot show its sources and its doubts.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Use Copilot Chat on the webIt searches the web when that helps.
  2. Pick the slower settingChoose Think deeper in the model selector for a more thorough answer.
  3. Demand sources and disagreementsUse the prompt below, then open the links.
  4. Collecting documents?Put them in a notebook, up to 50 sources, and question them together.
Prompt · Chat
Research [topic] using public web sources, for a decision on [decision].
Give me the key findings, where sources disagree, the risks, and a recommendation.
Cite every claim with its link. Mark anything you could not verify.

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Open the Researcher agentIn the Agents pane. It is slow on purpose and returns a cited report.
  2. Set the scopeWeb, your work sources, or both.
  3. Answer its questionsIt may ask before it starts. That steers the report.
Prompt · Premium
Research [topic] for a decision on [decision].
Scope: [the web only / our internal documents and emails only / both].
I need the key findings, where sources disagree, the risks, and a recommendation.
Cite every claim. Mark anything you could not verify.

Push it further

The first answer is a draft. Send one of these next:

Follow-up
What is the strongest argument against your recommendation?
Follow-up
Which of these sources is least reliable, and why?
Follow-up
Summarise this in five lines for someone senior.

Before you use it

  • Open the citations behind anything you will repeat.
  • A link is not proof. Check the page says what Copilot says it says.
  • Run it twice. Answers vary.

Results vary by licence and by your files. If a prompt misses, reply to Copilot with what to change.
