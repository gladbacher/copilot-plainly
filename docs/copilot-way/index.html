The Copilot way

Friends and articles talk about what they do with other AI tools. Your workplace gives you Copilot. Here is the nearest route for each thing, on your licence.

The short version

  • Most everyday jobs have a Copilot route on both licences.
  • Chat users get there by handing Copilot the material. Premium users can let it find the material.
  • Where I haven't checked a feature against Microsoft's pages yet, this page says so.

ChatOn Copilot Chat

You've heard of…The Copilot way
Chatting with an AI about anythingCopilot Chat at copilot.cloud.microsoft, signed in with your work account.
Uploading a document and asking about itUpload or select the file with your prompt. See Summarise a long document.
A slower "thinking" mode for hard questionsChoose Think deeper in the model selector.
Web research with sourcesAsk for cited findings in ordinary chat. See Research a topic. The Researcher agent needs a Copilot licence.
A custom assistant set up for one jobAn agent. Look for New agent in the Copilot app. On Chat it can use instructions and websites. See Make your own agent.
Help analysing dataPaste a small table or upload the file and ask for trends and formulas. See Make sense of a spreadsheet.
Making imagesThe Visual Creator agent, where your organisation has it.
An AI that reads your emailCopilot in Outlook works with your inbox and calendar on this licence. See Clear my inbox.
A writing or prompt coachWriting Coach and Prompt Coach agents, via All agents.

PremiumOn M365 Copilot (Premium)

You've heard of…The Copilot way
Chatting with an AI about anythingCopilot Chat, which can also draw on your emails, files, meetings and chats.
Uploading a document and asking about itName the file. No upload needed. See Summarise a long document.
A slower "thinking" mode for hard questionsChoose Think deeper in the model selector.
"Deep research" reportsThe Researcher agent: slow on purpose, with a cited report. See Researcher.
A custom assistant set up for one jobAn agent built in Agent Builder, which can use SharePoint, your email and Teams as knowledge. See Make your own agent.
Help analysing dataCopilot in Excel, or the Analyst agent. See Make sense of a spreadsheet.
Making imagesThe Visual Creator agent.
An AI that writes inside your documentsCopilot in Word, Excel and PowerPoint. See the app guides.
Meeting notes written for youCopilot in Teams meetings, where the organiser allows it. See Follow up a meeting.

Not checked yet

These come up often. I haven't verified them against Microsoft's pages for each licence, so they are not in the tables above:

  • A shared canvas or notebook that keeps a piece of work together across chats.
  • Memory and personal instructions that carry over between chats.
  • Saved prompts, and prompts that run on a schedule.
  • Voice conversations.

The quickest test is your own Copilot app: look in the left-hand menu and the prompt box for these options.
