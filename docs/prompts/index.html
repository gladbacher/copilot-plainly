Guide

The prompt library

Every prompt on this site in one searchable list. Fill in the blanks, copy, send.

The short version

  • A prompt has four parts: goal (required), context, source, expectations.
  • Replace everything in [square brackets].
  • If the first result is off, say what to change instead of starting again.
  • Search every prompt on the site, filtered to your licence.
About these prompts

They are built on Microsoft's four-part pattern and have not all been tested on every licence. Results differ by licence and by what is in your files, so treat each one as a starting point, fix it with a follow-up, and keep the ones that work for you.

Every prompt on this site

Search by job or app, or pick a group. With My licence set at the top, you only see prompts for your licence. Open Fill in the blanks under any prompt, type your details, then copy.

The recipe

PartWhat to sayRequired?
GoalThe task you want doneYes
ContextWhy you need it and who it is forNo
SourceWhat to draw on: your own pasted text, a file, or a web pageNo
ExpectationsFormat, tone, lengthNo

Habits worth keeping

  • Instruct it not to guess. "If it's not in the document, say so" works better than hoping.
  • Fix, don't restart. "Shorter, and less formal" is a complete follow-up.
  • Don't paste what you shouldn't. Read Safety first.
