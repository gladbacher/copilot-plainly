Guide 6

Teams meetings

Copilot can summarise a meeting while it happens and after it ends. Whether it can depends mostly on a setting the organiser chose.

The short version

  • The organiser controls Copilot for their meeting. If it's off, nobody can turn it on.
  • To ask questions after the meeting, transcription must have been on.
  • It doesn't work in meetings hosted by another organisation.
  • Check summaries against what you remember before you act on them or forward them.

1The organiser's setting

In Meeting options, under Copilot and other AI, the organiser picks one of three choices (Microsoft, 2026h):

ChoiceWhat it means
During and after the meetingTranscription is required. Copilot starts and stops with transcription, and works afterwards.
Only during the meetingCopilot uses speech-to-text and is available only live. Untranscribed speech and Copilot chats are not available after the meeting ends.
OffCopilot, recording and transcription are all disabled.

If you organise meetings, pick deliberately. If you attend them and want recaps, ask the organiser to choose the first option. Which choices are available can also depend on your company's policy, sensitivity labels and meeting templates.

2During the meeting

  • Copilot can summarise key discussion points, note who said what, suggest action items and answer questions in real time.
  • You talk to it in a private chat. Other attendees do not see your questions. Prompt suggestions appear under View prompts.
  • If you join more than 5 minutes late and Copilot is active, it offers a catch-up summary.
Prompt
What has been decided so far, and what is still open?
List any action items with the person responsible, if one was named.

3After the meeting

Open Copilot from the meeting chat or the Recap tab. Copilot cites its sources. Long answers (over 1,300 characters) can open in Word, and tables can open in Excel (Microsoft, 2026h).

Prompt
Write follow-up notes for the people who attended.
Include decisions, action items (owner and date if mentioned), and open questions.
Use only what was said in the meeting. If something is unclear, flag it instead of filling it in.

4Limits Microsoft lists

  • Doesn't work in meetings hosted outside your organisation.
  • After-meeting questions about spoken content need a transcript.
  • In a recurring meeting, earlier Copilot conversation history is lost if a later meeting in the series is transcribed. If the transcript is gone, Copilot is unavailable and history is removed.
  • Export to Word or Excel can be blocked by a sensitivity label or by the option that turns off copying and forwarding of chat, captions and transcripts.
  • Intelligent recap needs a Teams Premium add-on or a Copilot licence.
Be careful with people's words

Summaries can misattribute or soften what someone said. Check anything that affects a person, a decision or a customer. Microsoft also restricts Copilot from judging an employee's performance or emotional state from workplace communication (Microsoft, 2026d).
