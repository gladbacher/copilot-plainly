Playbook

Build a deck

Get the story right in outline form. Slides come second.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Outline in Copilot ChatPaste your notes and ask for a slide-by-slide outline.
  2. Tighten the storyAsk it to cut slides and sharpen titles until each title makes one point.
  3. Build the slides yourselfPaste titles and bullets into your organisation's template.
  4. Have Copilot in PowerPoint?If the Copilot button is there, follow the Premium route.
Prompt · Chat
Draft an outline for an [8]-slide presentation for [audience] about [topic].
Purpose: help them decide [decision].
For each slide give a title that makes one point, three short bullets, and one suggested visual.
Use only the notes below.

[Paste your notes here]

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Start from your templateOpen a new presentation from your organisation's template.
  2. Open Copilot, then Add ContentChoose Agent Mode. It may ask about audience and style, then shows an outline first.
  3. Or start from a Word documentIt pulls the structure and images across. Smaller files work best.
Prompt · Premium
Create an [8]-slide presentation for [audience] about [topic].
Purpose: help them decide [decision].
Structure: the problem, three options with pros and cons, a recommendation, next steps.
One idea per slide. Keep slide text short. Show me the outline before you build it.

Before you use it

  • Never trust a generated statistic. Trace each number.
  • Check images for brand and audience fit.
  • Cut. First drafts have too much text.
