Guide 10

When it breaks

Copilot not showing up, not seeing your files, or not answering. Start with the first one. It is the cause more often than not.

The short version

  • Most problems are the account (personal instead of work) or the licence (the thing you expected isn't included in yours).
  • Steps marked Microsoft come from Microsoft's documentation. Steps marked Reported are commonly suggested elsewhere; I have not confirmed them in Microsoft's pages.

1"I'm not sure I'm in the right Copilot"

Microsoft Work and personal accounts keep separate chats, files, and protections. In the work version you should see Work under your profile and a green data protection shield (Microsoft, 2026e).

  1. Sign out, then sign in at copilot.cloud.microsoft with your work email.
  2. Check the label under your name: Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Basic) or M365 Copilot (Premium) (Microsoft, 2026b).
  3. In Edge, make sure the browser profile itself is signed in with your work account.

2"There's no Copilot button in Word, Excel or PowerPoint"

Work down this list:

  1. Check your label. Copilot Chat (Basic) does not include Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint or OneNote (Microsoft, 2026a).
  2. Know the April 2026 report. Several university IT pages say embedded Copilot in these apps ended for people without a paid licence around then. Microsoft's overview still describes standard access for the Basic tier. If you lost it recently, ask IT before spending time on fixes.
  3. Ask IT to check your licence. Microsoft publishes a checker for administrators: Copilot License Details (Microsoft, 2026g).
  4. Reported In the app, open File > Account and look for Update License, then restart the app. The button is not always there.
  5. Reported Make sure the app is up to date. Some features are reported to be missing on the Semi-Annual Enterprise Channel; your update channel is shown under File > Account.
An admin setting can switch it off

Microsoft If your company turns off "connected experiences that analyze your content", Copilot is unavailable in Word, Excel, OneNote, Outlook and PowerPoint, regardless of licence (Microsoft, 2026d).

3"It says access is turned off"

Microsoft Administrators can block Copilot Chat for everyone or for chosen groups, including people with a paid licence. You may still see Chat in the app menu, but selecting it shows a message that access is disabled by policy. Your company can add a link there to its own guidance, titled View info from your organization (Microsoft, 2026e). Follow that link or ask IT.

Microsoft If the app won't load at all, IT may be blocking part of *.cloud.microsoft. Microsoft says all of that domain needs to be allowed (Microsoft, 2026e).

4"It can't find my files, emails or meetings"

  • Basic versions can't search your work data. Upload or paste the content, or open it in Outlook or Teams first (Microsoft, 2026a). This isn't a fault.
  • Premium can. If it still can't see something, check that you have permission to open that file yourself. Copilot only shows what you can access (Microsoft, 2026d).
  • Ask IT whether Work IQ has been switched off in your organisation. When it is off, answers aren't grounded in your work data (Microsoft, 2026a).

5"It invented something"

  1. Ask: "Where does that come from? Quote the source." Then open the citation.
  2. Re-run with an instruction such as "use only the attached document; if it isn't there, say so".
  3. Run it a second time. Microsoft notes results vary from run to run (Microsoft, 2026c).
  4. If it is wrong about something that matters, don't send it. Fix it by hand.

6"Copilot in my Teams meeting isn't working"

These come from Microsoft's page on Copilot in Teams meetings (Microsoft, 2026h).

SymptomLikely reason
Can't ask questions after the meetingThe organiser must have chosen "during and after the meeting", which needs transcription. Without a transcript, post-meeting questions about what was said don't work.
Copilot is missing entirelyThe organiser set Copilot to Off, which also disables recording and transcription. Only the organiser can change it (Online meeting options > Copilot and other AI).
It doesn't work in this meetingIt doesn't work in meetings hosted by another organisation.
No "intelligent recap"Recap needs a Teams Premium add-on or a Copilot licence.
Can't export an answer to Word or ExcelA sensitivity label or a "turn off copying and forwarding" meeting option can block it.
Joined lateIf you join more than 5 minutes after the start and Copilot is on, it offers a catch-up summary.

7What to send IT

You'll get a faster answer with this:

Message to IT
Hi, Copilot isn't working for me.
- App: [Word / Excel / Teams / copilot.cloud.microsoft]
- What I expected: [e.g. a Copilot button in Word]
- What happens: [e.g. no button / "access is turned off"]
- Label under my name at copilot.cloud.microsoft: [Copilot Chat (Basic) / M365 Copilot (Basic) / M365 Copilot (Premium) / none]
- Account I'm signed in with: [work email]
- Since when: [date, or "never worked"]
Can you check my licence assignment and any policies that apply to me?
