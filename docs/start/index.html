Quickstart · 2 minutes

Start here

Three steps. You finish knowing what you have and with one prompt that gives you something useful.

The short version

  • Go to copilot.cloud.microsoft and sign in with your work account, not a personal one.
  • Read the label under your name. It tells you which Copilot you have.
  • Give Copilot a goal, who it is for, and the format you want. Then refine it.
  1. Sign in with your work account Open copilot.cloud.microsoft. Use the email address your employer gave you. In the work version you should see the word Work under your profile and a green data protection shield. Personal accounts look different and have separate chats, files and protections (Microsoft, 2026a).
  2. Read your label Under your name you will see one of three labels: Copilot Chat (Basic), M365 Copilot (Basic) or M365 Copilot (Premium) (Microsoft, 2026b). Use the question below if you are unsure what yours means.
  3. Send one good prompt Copy the prompt below, change the brackets, and send it. If the first answer is not right, say what to change. Refining is normal.

What does your label say?

  • Copilot Chat (Basic): free chat that answers from the web. See Copilot Chat.
  • M365 Copilot (Basic): standard access in some apps. See what each version gives you.
  • M365 Copilot (Premium): the full paid add-on. See Safety.
  • No label: see Troubleshooting.

Your first prompt

Microsoft says a prompt has four parts: a goal (the only required one), context, a source and your expectations (Microsoft, 2026c). This one uses three of them.

Prompt
I need to explain [topic or decision] to [who: my manager / a client / my team].
They know [what they already know] and care about [what they care about].
Write a first draft in under 150 words, in a friendly and direct tone.
Then list the three questions they are most likely to ask.
Want it to use a document?

With Copilot Chat (Basic) you have to upload the file or select it with your prompt. Copilot does not go looking through your files for you. See giving it your files.
