Word and Outlook

Writing is where most people first get value. Both apps have Copilot, but they behave differently depending on your licence.

The short version

  • Word: describe what you want, optionally point to a source file, then keep, refine or discard the draft.
  • Outlook: Copilot Chat sits next to your inbox. Even without the paid licence it can work with your inbox and calendar.
  • Everything it writes is a draft. Check names, numbers, dates and anything you would be embarrassed to get wrong.

1Copilot in Word

Copilot in Word can draft from a request, an outline, notes or reference files; reuse the formatting of an existing template; add sections or tables; rewrite selected text; and add citations to the sources it used (Microsoft, 2026j).

Write a first draft

  1. Place the cursorOpen a blank document, or click where the new text should go.
  2. Open Copilot and describe the jobClick the Copilot icon at the bottom right. Say the topic, audience, tone, format and length.
  3. Add a source, if you want oneType / to choose a file Copilot should use.
  4. Generate, then decideKeep it, discard it, regenerate it, or refine it with a follow-up prompt.

Edit what you already have

Select text and ask Copilot to rewrite it, change the tone, shorten it or offer alternatives. You can also restructure a document in place with Edit with Copilot (Microsoft, 2026j).

Prompt
Rewrite the selected paragraph for a [client / executive / new starter] audience.
Keep it factual and under [80] words. Keep all dates and figures unchanged.
Check before you send

Microsoft's own list of things to verify: facts, numbers, citations, names, policy statements and other sensitive details. Copilot also applies the document's sensitivity label (Microsoft, 2026j).

Some Word features roll out gradually and depend on your licence, your organisation's settings, your app version and your platform. If a button in a screenshot is not on your screen, that is the usual reason.

2Copilot Chat in Outlook

In Outlook, Copilot is a chat panel beside your mail. Sign in with your work or school account and select the Copilot button near the top of the app. Microsoft lists support for Outlook for Microsoft 365, Outlook for Mac and New Outlook for Windows. Its page does not mention classic Outlook for Windows, so check yours (Microsoft, 2026l).

What it can do
Without the paid add-onAnswer questions about your inbox, calendar and meetings and other limited data. Take actions in Outlook: summarise emails, check meeting times, draft emails, schedule meetings, flag emails, set up automatic replies.
With the paid add-onAll of the above, plus your chats and enterprise data such as documents. Examples from Microsoft: prioritising unread email, drafting meeting agendas or pre-reads.

Source: Microsoft (2026l).

Useful email prompts

Prompt
Summarise my unread emails from this week into: needs a reply from me, for information only, and can wait.
List the sender and a one-line reason for each.
Prompt
Draft a reply to the open email. I want to [accept / decline / ask for more time].
Keep it under 100 words, polite and direct, with one clear next step.
Remember

Drafting an email is not sending one. Read it, then send it yourself. For prompts that work in both apps, see the prompt library.
