App guide

Word and Outlook

Writing is where most people first get value. Both apps have Copilot, but they behave differently depending on your tier.

The short version

  • Word: describe what you want, optionally point to a source file, then keep, refine or discard the draft.
  • Outlook: Copilot Chat sits next to your inbox and can work with your inbox and calendar on both tracks.
  • Everything it writes is a draft. Check names, numbers, dates and anything you would be embarrassed to get wrong.

For both tracks: check before you send

Check before you send

Microsoft's own list of things to verify: facts, numbers, citations, names, policy statements and other sensitive details. Copilot also applies the document's sensitivity label.

Some features roll out gradually and depend on your licence, your organisation's settings, your app version and your platform. If a button in a screenshot is not on your screen, that is the usual reason. Drafting an email is not sending one: read it, then send it yourself.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

Word

Microsoft says the Copilot Chat (Basic) label has no Copilot inside Word, while M365 Copilot (Basic) has standard access. Open Word and look for the Copilot icon at the bottom right to see which you have.

If you do not have it, you can still use Chat in your browser: paste the paragraph or upload the file, and ask for a rewrite.

Prompt · Chat
Rewrite the text below for a [client / executive / new starter] audience.
Keep it factual and under [80] words. Keep all dates and figures unchanged.

[Paste the text here]

Outlook

In Outlook, Copilot is a chat panel beside your mail. Sign in with your work or school account and select the Copilot button near the top of the app. Microsoft's Outlook support page lists Outlook for Microsoft 365, Outlook for Mac and New Outlook for Windows. Its Learn requirements page says Copilot works with "supported versions" of classic Outlook too, and needs an Exchange Online mailbox; Copilot isn't available in group mailboxes. Microsoft doesn't list which classic builds count, so the test is whether your Outlook shows the Copilot button.

On the Chat track, Copilot in Outlook can answer questions about your inbox, calendar and meetings and other limited data. It can take actions in Outlook: summarise emails, check meeting times, draft emails, schedule meetings, flag emails and set up automatic replies.

Prompt · Chat
Summarise my unread emails from this week into: needs a reply from me, for information only, and can wait.
List the sender and a one-line reason for each.
Prompt · Chat
Draft a reply to the open email. I want to [accept / decline / ask for more time].
Keep it under 100 words, polite and direct, with one clear next step.

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

Word

Copilot in Word can draft from a request, an outline, notes or reference files; reuse the formatting of an existing template; add sections or tables; rewrite selected text; and add citations to the sources it used.

  1. Place the cursorOpen a blank document, or click where the new text should go.
  2. Open Copilot and describe the jobClick the Copilot icon at the bottom right. Say the topic, audience, tone, format and length.
  3. Add a source, if you want oneType / to choose a file Copilot should use.
  4. Generate, then decideKeep it, discard it, regenerate it, or refine it with a follow-up prompt.

To edit what you already have, select text and ask Copilot to rewrite it, change the tone, shorten it or offer alternatives. You can also restructure a document in place with Edit with Copilot.

Prompt · Premium
Draft a one-page briefing on [topic] for [audience], using /[file name] and my recent emails with [person].
Keep it factual, with a short summary at the top and next steps at the end.

Outlook

Everything in the Chat track for Outlook, plus your chats and enterprise data such as documents. Microsoft's examples include prioritising unread email and drafting meeting agendas or pre-reads. The same version and mailbox requirements apply.

Prompt · Premium
Prepare me for my meeting with [person] tomorrow. Use our recent emails and shared files.
Give me a short agenda, the last three decisions we made, and anything outstanding.

If a button is missing, see Troubleshooting. More prompts are in the prompt library.
