Playbook

Write a first draft

Never start from a blank page. Start from an outline you agreed with Copilot.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Open Copilot ChatGo to copilot.cloud.microsoft, or the Copilot icon in the Edge sidebar.
  2. Agree the outline firstPaste your notes and send the prompt below. Fix the outline before any writing happens.
  3. Draft section by sectionSay "write section 1". Shorter requests give better text.
  4. Keep it in a PageUnder a good answer choose Edit in Pages to keep editing and share it, or paste it into Word.
  5. Have Copilot in Word?If your label is M365 Copilot (Basic), look for the Copilot button in Word and work there.
Prompt · Chat
I need to write [document type] for [audience] about [topic].
Here are my notes:
[Paste your notes here]
First give me an outline of five headings with one line under each. Wait for my OK before you write anything.

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Open Copilot in WordBlank document, then the Copilot icon at the bottom right.
  2. Point it at your sourcesType / to pick a file. It can also use your recent emails.
  3. Generate, then steerKeep, regenerate or refine with a follow-up. Select any paragraph to rewrite just that part.
Prompt · Premium
Draft [document type] for [audience] about [topic], using /[file name] and my recent emails with [person].
Start with a three-line summary. Use short headings. Keep it to [one page].
Mark anything you were unsure of with the word CHECK.

Push it further

The first answer is a draft. Send one of these next:

Follow-up
Rewrite the opening three ways: formal, friendly and blunt.
Follow-up
What would a sceptical reader challenge? List the three weakest points.
Follow-up
Cut this by a third without losing any number or date.

Before you use it

  • Facts, figures, names and policy statements: verify each one.
  • Does it sound like you? Rewrite the first and last paragraph yourself.
  • Check the sensitivity label on the finished document.

Results vary by licence and by your files. If a prompt misses, reply to Copilot with what to change.
