Playbook

Clear my inbox

Let Copilot sort the pile, then answer the three emails that matter. Sorting post works the same way: bills, birthday cards and junk mail each get different handling.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Open Copilot in OutlookSelect the Copilot button near the top of Outlook. On the Chat licence it can still work with your inbox and calendar.
  2. Triage firstSend the prompt below. Don't reply to anything yet.
  3. Draft one reply at a timeOpen the email, then ask for a draft. Read it, fix it, send it yourself.
Prompt · Chat
Summarise my unread emails from [this week] into three groups: needs a reply from me, for information only, and can wait.
For each one give the sender and a one-line reason.
Then name the three I should answer first, and why.

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Open Copilot in OutlookSame button. With your licence it can also draw on your Teams chats and documents.
  2. Triage with contextSend the prompt below. It can check what was said elsewhere before you reply.
  3. Draft, check, sendAsk for a draft of each reply. Read it, fix it, send it yourself.
Prompt · Premium
Summarise my unread emails from [this week] into three groups: needs a reply from me, for information only, and can wait.
For the three most urgent, check related files and Teams chats and say what I need to know before I reply.
Name the source of each point.

Push it further

The first answer is a draft. Send one of these next:

Follow-up
Draft a reply to the first one. I want to [accept / decline / ask for more time]. Under 100 words, one clear next step.
Follow-up
Which of these are waiting on someone else, and since when?
Follow-up
Flag the ones that can wait until Friday.

Before you use it

  • Copilot drafts. You send. Read every reply before it goes.
  • Check names, dates and anything you are promising.
  • A summary can miss the one line that matters. Open anything important.

Results vary by licence and by your files. If a prompt misses, reply to Copilot with what to change.
