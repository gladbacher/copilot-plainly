Guide 5

PowerPoint

Copilot can give you a working first deck in minutes. Treat it as a draft you shape, not a finished product.

The short version

  • Start from a prompt or from a Word document.
  • It may ask about audience and style, then show an outline before building slides.
  • Start from your company template, and keep source Word files under 24 MB.
  • Edit and review. Microsoft says AI output needs a person to check it.

1Two ways to start

Inside PowerPoint

  1. Open a new presentationIdeally from your organisation's template.
  2. Open CopilotClick the Copilot icon at the bottom right, then Add Content.
  3. Choose Agent Mode and enter your promptCopilot may ask clarifying questions about audience and style, and builds an outline you can refine before it creates the slides.

In the Copilot app

Sign in at m365.cloud.microsoft with your work or school account, ask Copilot to create a presentation on your topic, and confirm when prompted. It saves the deck to your OneDrive and gives you a link (Microsoft, 2026k).

2Turning a Word document into a deck

Copilot can pull a Word document's structure and images into slides. Microsoft's tips (2026k):

  • It works best with files under 24 MB.
  • Starting from your organisation's template gives the best results.
  • You need access and permission to use the file you point it to.

3A prompt to start with

Prompt
Create a [8]-slide presentation for [audience] about [topic].
Purpose: help them decide [decision].
Structure: the problem, three options with pros and cons, a recommendation, and next steps.
Tone: plain and confident. One main idea per slide, and keep slide text short.

4Limits Microsoft lists

  • Prompt-based creation supports one output language at a time.
  • When you create from a file, you can't add extra context in the same prompt.
  • Referencing files in Agent Mode is listed as "coming soon".
  • Controls for length, tone, style and image preferences are rolling out first to Frontier and Microsoft 365 Insiders, then to all Copilot subscribers later.
  • AI-generated images are labelled "AI-generated" in the slide's speaker notes.
Licence

You need a Copilot licence included with your subscription, and your organisation's settings must allow it. If you don't see Copilot, check which licence you have (Microsoft, 2026k). Start with Start here.

5Before you present

  • Read every slide for facts and numbers. Don't trust generated statistics.
  • Check images: do they fit your brand and your audience?
  • Cut. First drafts almost always have too much text.
