Follow up a meeting

Notes out within the hour, with owners and dates, and nothing invented. A meeting without written follow-up is a conversation that politely evaporates.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Get the wordsCopy your notes, or the transcript from the meeting chat if one was made.
  2. Paste them into Copilot ChatIn Teams, Copilot Chat can also see the item you have open.
  3. Ask for actions as a tableTell it to write "not stated" instead of guessing an owner.
Prompt · Chat
Below are the notes from our meeting. Write follow-up notes for the people who attended.
Include decisions, action items as a table (action, owner, due date), and open questions.
Use only what is in the notes. Write "not stated" if an owner or date is missing.

[Paste the notes here]

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Open the meeting recapFrom the meeting chat or the Recap tab in Teams.
  2. Ask Copilot thereIt works from the transcript, so the organiser must have allowed Copilot during and after the meeting.
  3. Send the notes yourselfLong answers can open in Word.
Prompt · Premium
Write follow-up notes for the people who attended this meeting.
Include decisions, action items as a table (action, owner, due date), and open questions.
Use only what was said. Write "not stated" if an owner or date is missing, and quote the line each action came from.

Before you use it

  • Summaries can misattribute or soften what someone said. Check anything about a person.
  • Confirm owners and dates with the people named.
  • No transcript, no after-meeting answers. See the organiser's setting.

Results vary by licence and by your files. If a prompt misses, reply to Copilot with what to change.
