Playbooks

What do you want to get done?

Twelve everyday jobs. Each one gives the best route on your licence, a prompt you can fill in and copy, follow-ups to push the answer further, and what to check before you use it.

  • Clear my inboxTriage unread mail, draft replies, flag what can wait.
  • Write a first draftFrom rough notes to a structured draft you can edit.
  • Shorten or sharpenMake existing text shorter, clearer or better pitched.
  • Summarise a long documentGet the point, the risks and the gaps from a long file.
  • Prepare for a meetingAgenda, questions and likely objections in five minutes.
  • Follow up a meetingDecisions, actions and owners, sent while it is fresh.
  • Make sense of a spreadsheetTrends, outliers and formulas without breaking the file.
  • Build a deckA structured first deck from a prompt or a document.
  • Research a topicA sourced answer you can check, not a confident guess.
  • Plan a piece of workSteps, risks and a first week, pressure-tested.
  • Learn something fastA personal tutor for a topic, a tool or a piece of jargon.
  • Catch up after time awayBack from leave: what happened, what is waiting on you.

Set My licence at the top of the page and each playbook shows only your route. Not sure which you have? Start here.
