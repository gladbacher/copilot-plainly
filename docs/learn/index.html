Playbook

Learn something fast

Ask to be taught, then ask to be tested.

ChatWith Copilot Chat

  1. Say what you know alreadyIt pitches the explanation to you.
  2. Ask for a quizBeing tested is what makes it stick.
  3. Have reading material?Put the files in a notebook and ask for a study guide, flashcards or a quiz.
  4. Try a coach agentOpen Agents, then All agents, and look for Learning Coach or Prompt Coach. What you see depends on your organisation.
Prompt · Chat
Teach me [topic]. I already know [what you know] and I need it for [why].
Explain it in plain words with one worked example from [my kind of work].
Then ask me five questions, one at a time, and tell me what I got wrong.

PremiumWith M365 Copilot (Premium)

  1. Say what you know alreadyIt pitches the explanation to you.
  2. Add how your organisation does itAsk it to use internal documents on the topic that you can open.
  3. Ask for a quizBeing tested is what makes it stick.
Prompt · Premium
Teach me [topic]. I already know [what you know] and I need it for [why].
Explain it in plain words, then show how we handle it here, using our internal documents on [topic].
Then ask me five questions, one at a time, and tell me what I got wrong.

Before you use it

  • It can teach a mistake confidently. Check key facts against a trusted source.
  • For anything regulated, your organisation's own guidance wins.
  • If the quiz feels too easy, ask for harder questions.

Results vary by licence and by your files. If a prompt misses, reply to Copilot with what to change.
