## Copilot Pages (ba)
- Turn a chat response into an editable, shareable page. Below a response: ellipsis > "Edit in Pages" (new or existing page), or the pencil icon. From a new chat: plus button > More > "Draft page or code".
- Work accounts: available to those with SharePoint or OneDrive storage, "including ones without a Microsoft 365 Copilot license".
- Pages stay until deleted; earlier pages are in the Copilot Library. Admin may control tools and data sources in Pages. No limits stated. No page date.

## Copilot Notebooks (bb, bc)
- A workspace that gathers chat, files, pages, meeting notes and links for one task; shareable. Needs a Copilot or Copilot Chat licence and a SharePoint or OneDrive service plan.
- Create: m365.cloud.microsoft > Notebooks (or via the app launcher) > All notebooks > New notebook > name it > add references (search, Upload, OneDrive, drag and drop) > Create.
- Chat vs licensed (bb): sources per notebook 50 vs 300; suggested references and suggested notebooks not available vs included; Researcher not available vs up to 25 a month; one-shot Word/Excel/PowerPoint document not available vs included; mind maps, infographics, study guides, reports, flashcards and quizzes standard vs priority processing; Audio Overviews not available vs up to 20 a day; Live Capture not available vs included.
- Reference types for both: PDFs, text, media (.jpeg, .png, .mp4), Word/Excel/PowerPoint files, OneNote and Loop pages, OneNote sections, email threads with attachments, .md/.txt/.rtf. Licensed only: URLs, Teams chats, meeting invites, meeting chats/transcripts/summaries, files shared in meetings, OneNote notebooks, SharePoint and OneDrive.
- Unofficial: a Microsoft Q&A thread (late Sept 2026) reports unlicensed users getting a licence error in the Notebooks side panel. Not confirmed by Microsoft.

## Memory and custom instructions (bd, be, bh)
- Copilot Memory saves key details from chats; "available to Copilot Chat users with and without a Microsoft Copilot license" (bh). Custom instructions: licence not stated.
- Ask "What do you know about me". Temporary chat: "Start a new temporary chat", top right; nothing is saved to memory, but organisational retention may still keep it and admins may access it during retention (bd).
- Custom instructions: "... Settings and more" > Chat settings > Personalization > Custom instructions > Edit instructions > Save instructions (be).
- Admin: "Enhanced personalization" control, on by default. When off, users see Custom instructions, Saved memories and Chat history as off and cannot turn them on (bh). Memories are stored in the user's Exchange mailbox; admins can search them with eDiscovery (bh).
- Work insights depend on the organisation's Copilot plan (bd).

## Saved prompts (bf)
- After a prompt has run, hover over it > bookmark icon > "Save prompt". Appears under "Your saved prompts" in the Copilot Prompt Lab; visible only to you. The page's "Applies to" lists Word and PowerPoint; licence not stated.

## Scheduled prompts (bg)
- Hover over a prompt > "Schedule this prompt" > choose timing and email notification > Save. Up to 10. Manage under "... Settings and more" > Scheduled prompts. "A Microsoft Copilot license is required." On by default unless optional connected experiences are off.
